One ring per plant, in its own flower colour, starting at January and turning clockwise. The outer band counts how many plants are in flower each month.

Layers

A side view of one zone, drawn to scale at mature size. Drag a plant along the ground to try a position; positions stay on this device. Change the month to see what's in flower and what's bare.

Scale: one square of the ground grid is one metre. Trees taller than 9 m run off the top.
